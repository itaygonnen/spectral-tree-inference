\section{Main Result}\label{sec:main}
The entry-wise recovery criterion \eqref{eq:entrywise} is what the sampling rate
has to buy. This section converts it into a sufficient condition on $p$, first
for the Laplacian $L=D-S$ and then for the centred distance operator $\Bop$,
stated throughout in the structural quantities of \Cref{sec:bridging}: the
margin $\rho$ of \eqref{eq:margin_definition} and the imbalance $\eta$ of
\eqref{eq:imbalance_ratio}.

\begin{theorem}[Exact top-down split recovery]\label{thm:main-sim}
In a non-balanced CBM with $m$ terminal nodes and imbalance $\eta\ge1$, let $\rho$
be the structural margin and let $\vvh$ be the empirical Fiedler vector --- the
unit eigenvector belonging to the second-smallest eigenvalue of the sampled
Laplacian $\hat L=\hat D-\hat S$, formed from entries observed with probability
$p$ as in \Cref{asm:sampling}. Assume
\Cref{asm:gtr,asm:clock,asm:cbm,asm:bounded,asm:margin,asm:sampling}. For the
top-down partition to succeed (i.e.\ $\norm{\vvh-\vv}_\infty<\min_i|\vv_i|$), it
suffices that
\begin{equation}\label{eq:main_rate}
   p\;>\;\frac{8(1+\eta)^3\,\bze^2\,\log m}{m\,(\rho-\eta\,\Sout^{\max})^2}
       \;=\;\Theta\!\left(\frac{\log m}{m}\right),
\end{equation}
in which case $\operatorname{sign}(\vvh)$ recovers $(A,B)$ exactly \hp.
\end{theorem}

\noindent The distance operator $\Bop=H\Dmat H$ of \Cref{sec:problem} admits a
statement of the same shape --- entry-wise sign agreement of an empirical
structural eigenvector against a piecewise-constant target, at a rate governed by
a spectral gap. Since the hypotheses of the two operators are not nested, the
following is a \emph{sibling} of \Cref{thm:main-sim} rather than a corollary of
it.
