\section{Extension: the Hierarchical Block Model}\label{app:hbm}
Here \Cref{asm:cbm} is replaced by \Cref{asm:hbm}; \Cref{asm:gtr,asm:clock,asm:bounded}
are retained. The CBM holds exactly under the strict clock with \emph{symmetric}
clans; for asymmetric (caterpillar-like) clans, within-clan MRCA depths vary even
under the clock, so intra-clan entries are no longer constant. The HBM of
\Cref{asm:hbm} retains the constant cross-clan boundary but lets intra-clan
similarities decay geometrically with path depth; its place in the chain of
generative assumptions is recorded in \Cref{tab:assumption_chain}. This appendix
draws the two consequences: a geometric floor on the weakest intra-clan
similarity, and the spectral-gap bound that floor induces.

\begin{proposition}[Geometric floor]\label{prop:D-floor}
Under \Cref{asm:hbm}, $\Sin^{\min}\ge\Sin\,\alpha^{D_{\max}}$ with
$D_{\max}=\lceil\log_2\max(a,b)\rceil$.
\end{proposition}
\begin{proof}
GTR multiplicativity along paths gives
$S_{ij}=\prod_{e\in\mathrm{path}(i,j)}\alpha_e$, so a same-clan pair at depth
$\le D_{\max}$ satisfies $S_{ij}\ge\Sin\,\alpha^{D_{\max}}$; minimising over
same-clan pairs gives the floor, with $D_{\max}$ the height of the deeper clan
subtree.
\end{proof}

\begin{proposition}[HBM spectral-gap bound]\label{prop:D-gap}
Substituting \Cref{prop:D-floor} into \Cref{lem:gap} gives
\[
   \Dlam\ge m_{\min}\bigl(\Sin\alpha^{D_{\max}}-\Sout^{\max}\bigr)-\eta\,m_{\min}\Sout^{\max}
        =m_{\min}\Sin\alpha^{D_{\max}}-(1+\eta)m_{\min}\Sout^{\max},
\]
positive iff $\alpha^{D_{\max}}>(1+\eta)\Sout^{\max}/\Sin$. As $\alpha\to1$ (or
$D_{\max}=0$) this reduces to \Cref{lem:gap} and the CBM margin condition of
\Cref{asm:margin}.
\end{proposition}

\Cref{fig:hbm_spectral_verification} verifies the bound on simulated HBM matrices,
and \Cref{fig:fiedler_partitions} illustrates the imbalance-driven degradation of
the recovered partition.
