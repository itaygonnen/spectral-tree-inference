### [B1/29] `lem:C-first`'s Weyl step is false at ℓ=1 on the similarity side

- **Item:** `lem:C-first` asserts `|λ_ℓ - λ̂₂| ≥ Δλ - ‖E_L‖₂` for all ℓ ≠ 2. With `λ₁ = 0` and
